# Clinic Brand and Website

A complete visual identity, print kit, social templates and responsive Arabic website for a cardiology and clinical-nutrition centre.

**Status:** delivered and live · **Repo:** private

## Highlights

- Right-to-left, Arabic-first design with a documented design-token system (colors, type scale, spacing) and WCAG AA contrast.
- Print kit: business cards, appointment cards, prescription pad, letterhead.
- Social kit: service posts, opening announcement, stories, cover, messaging signature.
- Site with security headers, sitemap and structured data for search and AI answer engines.
- Auto-deploy from Git to a managed host.

## Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, design tokens, Render.
